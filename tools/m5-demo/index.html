What do these words mean?
M5Stack CoreS3
A small programmable device with a touchscreen. This project gives two of them different jobs.
Brain & Controller
The Brain handles settings and audio data. The Controller turns those instructions into lighting output.
DMX
A control protocol for stage lighting. Here its values only drive virtual lights in your browser.
Master & blackout
Master means overall brightness. Blackout temporarily sets every lighting output value to zero.
Audio bars & BPM
The bars show low through high frequency levels. BPM means beats per minute; the generated beat here is 120 BPM and makes no sound.
Connection
The real project uses ESP-NOW for wireless messages. This demo uses a software model of that link.

Everything happens in your browser, using simulated devices, sound data, and lights. This demonstrates the software’s behavior; real audio input, wireless connections, and physical lights need separate testing. Each lesson starts fresh so you can repeat it.
